Our honey

Two harvests, two different seasons. The same apiary, the same bees: all that changes is what is in bloom around them while the honey is made.

The two lines

Spring Line

Spring wildflower

Harvested in spring

Pale and delicate, made from the first blossom of the year. Floral on the nose, light on the palate. In time it crystallises and turns creamy: that is normal, and it is the proof it was never heated.

Summer Line

Summer wildflower

Harvested in summer

Darker and bolder, from the flowering of the hot season. Full flavoured, with the faintly bitter note typical of Mediterranean herbs. It stays liquid longer than the spring one.
