Package bees

A package contains young bees and is the ideal solution for a range of beekeeping needs. It can be used with a queen to establish a new colony, or without a queen to boost existing colonies ahead of the spring build-up.

It is a lightweight solution, ideal both for starting a new operation or expanding your apiary and for strengthening your hives before the season begins, with low shipping costs.

We prepare packages to order and tell you when to install them and the first steps for starting or strengthening the colony.
