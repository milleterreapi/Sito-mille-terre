Anatolian

Apis mellifera anatoliaca
Breeder queen
  • code 355 · Ivan B. Nielsen
  • Thrifty
  • Winters well
  • Hardy

An Anatolian line selected for ease of management and the robustness of its colonies. It stands out for the low consumption of stores through winter and for its ability to build populous colonies, holding a good population through the season. An interesting genetic for beekeepers looking for efficiency in management and steady hive development.

Caucasian

Apis mellifera caucasica
Breeder queen
  • code 167 · Ivan B. Nielsen
  • Very gentle
  • Long tongue
  • Strong forager

Valued for its generally gentle temperament and its characteristically long tongue, the Caucasian is of interest for working nectar sources other bees can barely reach. A line worth considering for gentleness, foraging ability and the way it manages its stores. The right choice for a beekeeper who wants to widen what their apiary can do through specialised genetics.

Athos 344

Selected line · dark phenotype
Breeder queen
  • code 344 · Ivan B. Nielsen
  • Dark phenotype
  • Productive
  • Easy to manage
  • Fast spring build-up

A genetic line of interest to beekeepers looking for productive, manageable colonies. Swarming tendency, together with the speed of spring build-up and the management of space in the brood nest, is a key parameter in assessing this line. Breeder queen 344 carries the darker phenotype of the line: brown thorax and an abdomen with pronounced dark banding.
